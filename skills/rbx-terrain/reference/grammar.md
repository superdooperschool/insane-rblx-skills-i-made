# Terrain grammar - measured from the user's areas (rolling-ball game)

Two kinds of numbers live here and they are kept apart:
- **Targets** = what the kit must produce. Each one cites an user verdict (feedback.md round) or a scan cell,
  and names the kit default or test that enforces it. User verdicts win over user-area measurements: the
  the user's own areas contain steep decorative cones (A1 hills worst approach 43 deg, A3 51 deg) that the
  user rejected when the kit built them (round 5, round 7).
- **Measured** = the generated table from `run.sh analyze` (scripts/analyze). Never type these by hand: rerun
  the analyzer and `node scripts/analyze/table.mjs --into reference/grammar.md <scans...>.json`.

## Why several grass materials

1. `GlobalShadows=false`: shape barely shades, so paint IS the shading. Light Limestone on crests and
   Leafy tone breaks in hollows make relief readable.
2. GrassLOD grows blades on Grass/LeafyGrass/Limestone/Cobblestone/Slate (blocked Rock/Ground/Mud, max
   55 deg) and colours each blade with its material's terrain colour: one blade system, many tones.
3. SwordService pays only on Grass/LeafyGrass/Limestone: grass-family = income ground, Mud lanes = bare
   fast strips, Rock = nothing.

## Air (why it is there)

Deliberate every time: arch over a lane (A2), bores through mounds (A4), undercut crater rim (A5),
undercut strata ledges on cliffs (A1, T1 gorge). It gives go-under / go-over choices and makes cliffs read.

## Boundaries a human sees

Basalt/Rock cliffs, strata ledges (Grass tops high up on cliffs - excluded by height band), gorges with
air under, water, the box edge, zone gates / level blocks. Terrain work stays inside, 40 studs off them.

## Pro-builder rules (DevForum tutorials, cross-checked against A4 numbers)

- Macro first, blend second: place a few big masses, then soften edges (Grow/blur). In the kit: few large
  pieces with cosine edges that reach zero slope over 60-80 studs; never many small bumps.
- Paint by slope: rock on faces steeper than ~35 deg, grass on gentler ground (A4: Basalt owns 45-70 deg,
  LeafyGrass 20-45, Grass under 20).
- Paint broad uniform areas; hand-painted small details read as noise. No lip/rim bands; Limestone as whole
  crest caps and whole lane floors (A4 largest Limestone patch 15k studs2).
- Dirt only where grass would not grow: bore floors, under overhangs, deep hollows (A4 Mud sits 17 below local mean).
- Flow: the landscape should lead the eye and the ball along one line; avoid evenly repeated placements.
